SQ / MANIFESTO — READ BEFORE BOARDING

Why Us

life at 21 is already a full-time job you didn't apply for. classes, work, family, the algorithm telling you you're behind.

so we didn't hire a company to fix that for us, we built our own two hours off the grid.

no event planners, no brand deck, no sponsors calling the shots. just us setting up the speakers because nothing like this existed yet.

we're not another thing on your to-do list. we're the thing you actually look forward to — built by the same people who needed it.

It’s all about not being ordinary!

SQ / ORIGIN STORY

About Us

honestly? we're just a bunch of people in our early 20s who realised that adulting hit different and nobody warned us.

the stress, the routines, the pressure of figuring everything out — it gets heavy. and somewhere in between all of that, the fun stopped being a priority.

we started The Sidequest because we needed it ourselves. a night where none of that exists. where you walk in, the music is right, the people are right, and for a few hours you're not a student or an employee or someone's responsibility — you're just there.
